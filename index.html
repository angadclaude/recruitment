How to use this in coaching

  • Show each coach their own block, the league table and the leadership section. Start with the verdict line, walk the drop-off points name by name, then finish on leaders advanced — the positive proof that development works.
  • Left early (red) is the priority — a start who never reaches their first milestone (2 or 4 sign-ups) signals a broken first week: no owner attention, weak first-day field experience, or a booking that never should have been made.
  • Stalled (amber) means they activated but plateaued. Find where they got stuck and whether they were still being run daily after the early incentive was banked.
  • Retained / cleared (green) and ★ leaders are what good looks like — get the coach to articulate exactly what they did so it's repeatable.
  • Volume vs retention vs leaders: read every coach on three axes — do they feed the funnel, hold their people, and turn them into leaders? Monish and Angad score on all three; the team needs more coaches doing the same.
  • Data notes: LINK only. Leadership figures are Q2 2026 from the live tracker and include HF-campaign starts (e.g. Mo Aden's Mubarak, Phinellah). Days-to-leadership uses recorded start week; very short gaps are experienced transfers. Horace sits in Daria's drop-off block but his leadership is credited to Angad on the tracker. Mo Aden's new starts are pulled from the HF campaign (tagged HF) plus his early FR starts, so his numbers reflect his full recruit base.
